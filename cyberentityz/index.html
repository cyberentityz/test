Ask a question
01

How are your VAPT and training fees structured?

Our services are strictly scoped around defined deliverables — never open-ended hourly billing. VAPT engagements have fixed scope with included free re-testing, while college bootcamps and training programs are priced per cohort with clear curriculum milestones.

02

How quickly can CYBERENTITYZ start an engagement?

For critical security assessments or incident response, our red team initiates within 24 to 48 hours following mutual NDA execution. For academic workshops and lab setup consulting, scheduling is typically confirmed within 5 business days.

03

Who from your team conducts the penetration tests and training?

All assessments and sessions are conducted by OSCP, CEH, and CISSP-certified practitioners with active field experience in real-world exploit development, defense evasion, and vulnerability research — never junior delegates.

04

Do you assist with developer remediation and re-testing?

Yes. Every assessment delivers actionable developer patch snippets and remediation roadmaps. We conduct complimentary verification re-tests within 30 days to certify that all identified vulnerabilities have been securely resolved.

05

Is proprietary client information and data strictly confidential?

Unconditionally. All penetration testing engagements and institutional collaborations operate under comprehensive non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with end-to-end encrypted telemetry and secure report delivery protocols.
